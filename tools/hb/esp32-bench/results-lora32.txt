Relay pipeline benchmark of the spec 11 reference frame (tools/hb, make hb-test) on a LILYGO LoRa32 v2.1 (ESP32-PICO-D4, 240 MHz), 31 October 2026.
Build: -O2, the faster build_freq (fast_freq.h, byte-identical output), model tables read from flash, the benchmark in its own 24 KB task.
50 fabricated packets: 36 varied, the 12 longest of the 500 vectors, and two hand-made worst cases (a 253-character text; a 7-element path). 10 repetitions each, mean per packet.
"relay" is hb_relay, which first verifies the frame (decode, CRC, re-encode check) and then rewrites the path, copies the content and appends the tuple.

                      mean       max
encode (own packet)   11.5 ms    47.2 ms   (hb_encode also decodes what it built, to check the round trip)
receive (verify)       5.5 ms    23.8 ms
relay hop (complete)   7.4 ms    24.2 ms   (31 of the 50 packets are relayable)

WiFi off vs on (soft AP and a background scan on the other core): identical to 0.1 ms.
Longest frame in the set: 239 bytes (253-character text): receive 23.8 ms, relay 24.2 ms; SF7, BW 125 kHz, CR 4/5 airtime of that frame 374 ms (a 255-byte frame: 400 ms).
Worst relay against the airtime of its own frame: about 10% (a 117-byte frame: 20 ms of work, 195 ms on the air). The firmware printed 20.1% for the worst "receive + relay", which counts the verification twice.
Stack: the benchmark task used 10,376 bytes. The default Arduino loop task has 8,192 and overflowed on the first try, so firmware must run this code in a task with at least 12 KB.
Free heap during the run: 283 KB at start, 226 KB minimum (the board was not connected to WiFi as a client).
Not measured: the T-Deck (the flash was not allowed), a client WiFi connection with APRS-IS traffic, the real iGate task load.
